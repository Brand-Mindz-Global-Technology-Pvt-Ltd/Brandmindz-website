Case Study: Driving High-Quality Admissions for an Arts & Science College
Industry
Education – Arts & Science College
Campaign Objective
Generate high-intent student and parent inquiries in a highly competitive region and convert them into confirmed admissions.
Campaign Duration
5 Months
Marketing Channel
Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

The Challenge
The college was facing increasing competition from multiple institutions in the same region. While they had strong academic programs and student support systems, their digital presence was not translating into quality admission inquiries.
Key challenges included:
Intense regional competition for the same student pool


Low-quality or low-intent leads from generic education ads


Difficulty gaining parent trust through digital platforms


Rising cost of student acquisition


Need to promote new-age courses alongside traditional programs


The requirement was not just more leads — it was better leads that could convert into actual admissions.

Our Strategy
Instead of running generic “Admissions Open” campaigns, we designed a trust-first, outcome-driven admission strategy.
Our approach focused on answering the real questions students and parents care about:
What will my career look like after graduation?


Is this college future-ready?


Will my child receive proper guidance and support?


Key Strategic Pillars
Career Growth Storytelling
 Highlighted the career progression of past students to build credibility and trust.


Future-Ready Course Positioning
 Promoted newly introduced AI-related programs to attract students looking beyond conventional degrees.


Parent-Focused Messaging
 Separate communication for parents, focusing on student care, mentorship, discipline, and academic support.


Student-Centric Communication
 Showcased campus life, learning environment, and career opportunities.


Quality-First Lead Funnel
 All leads were collected through a website landing page, ensuring only Sales Qualified Leads (SQLs) and avoiding junk or low-interest inquiries.



Execution
Meta ad campaigns tailored for students and parents


Multiple creatives and messaging angles tested over 5 months


Course-wise segmentation to attract relevant prospects


Landing page designed to filter intent before submission


Continuous optimization to improve lead quality and conversion



Performance Results
Total Campaign Budget: ₹1,24,587.06


Sales Qualified Leads Generated: 550


Average Cost per SQL: ~₹226


Admission Conversion Rate: 20%


Confirmed Admissions: ~110 students


Lead Source: Website landing page (High intent only)



Impact Delivered
📈 Built a strong, high-quality admission pipeline


🎯 Achieved meaningful conversions, not just inquiries


👨‍👩‍👧 Improved parent confidence and counselor engagement


🤖 Positioned the college as career-focused and future-ready


🚀 Reduced dependence on low-quality third-party lead sources



Client Outcome
This campaign helped the college move away from volume-driven marketing and adopt a quality-first digital admission strategy. By focusing on trust, outcomes, and future relevance, the institution was able to convert digital interest into real admissions—even in a highly competitive region.
“We didn’t just generate leads. We helped the institution attract the right students and parents.”
