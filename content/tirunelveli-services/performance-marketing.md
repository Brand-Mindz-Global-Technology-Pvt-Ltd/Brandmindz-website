### **What's the difference between performance marketing and digital marketing?**

Digital marketing is a broad term that covers activities such as SEO, social media marketing, content marketing, branding, email marketing, and paid advertising.

Performance marketing focuses more specifically on measurable results, such as leads, sales, conversions, and return on advertising spend.

### **How quickly can I see results from paid advertising?**

Paid advertising can start generating clicks and enquiries relatively quickly, sometimes within the first few days.

However, the initial results are not always enough to determine what will work best. Campaigns usually need time for testing and optimisation before you can make stronger decisions about performance.
